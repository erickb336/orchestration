# ORC-007: Fan-out: breakdown steps that create tasks, and parallel agents per step

## Task identity

- ID and title: ORC-007, fan-out at both levels: steps that break work into child tasks, and several agents on one step
- Author and timestamps: Lead (Claude), 2026-09-29
- Status, priority, and priority rationale: Proposed, P1. The user asked whether a plan or design step can create multiple tasks, and whether each pipeline step can have parallel agents. Both serve the user's goal of using Claude and Codex capacity at large scale.
- Specification revision: 2

## Problem and outcome

Today only the lead creates tasks, and each step is exactly one agent run. Large goals therefore serialise inside a task, and a design cannot hand its work items straight to parallel execution.

Outcome:

- A step can produce a **task breakdown**. The service turns each valid item into a child task linked to its parent, and the children run concurrently.
- A step can run **several agents at once**:
  - **Independent copies**, whose outputs are combined.
  - **Best of N**, where a later step chooses one of the candidates.

Everything stays visible, pausable, and editable, like any other step.

## Scope

Included:

- **Breakdown artifacts** (a new artifact kind, `breakdown`). Items are validated like lead proposals: title, outcome, acceptance, template, optional dependencies between items. On acceptance, each item becomes a child task (`parentTaskId`, IDs `<parent>.1`, `<parent>.2`, …).
  - The parent's later steps may wait for all children with `waitFor: "children"`.
  - A review gate on the breakdown step lets the user edit the list before any child exists.
- **Per-step parallelism:** `parallel: { count: 2–5, mode: "copies" | "best-of" }`, with optional per-copy provider/model overrides (for example one Claude and one Codex copy).
  - *Copies:* every copy must succeed. Outputs become `name#1..n`, and later steps receive all of them. Review findings are summed for run-if conditions.
  - *Best of N:* writers work on separate branches. The next step receives every candidate and must name one in its output (`chosen`). Only the chosen change flows further, and the others stay on their branches.
- **Controls:** pause, stop, stale-result, and revalidation rules apply to every copy. Provider limits count every copy.
- **UI:**
  - Pipeline editor fields for parallelism and breakdown.
  - Parent and child links on tasks.
  - Candidate comparison and choice in artifacts.
- **Tests** for the domain and scheduler with scripted adapters.

- **Iteration (user direction, r2: "It should iterate"):**
  - *Step loops.* A pipeline may mark a group of steps (for example review → repair) as a loop. The loop repeats until its exit condition holds (the review reports no open findings) or `maxIterations` is reached, and every iteration's artifacts are kept as versions.
  - *Goal loops.* After a breakdown's children settle, an evaluation step (the lead, or a designated reviewer) checks the parent's acceptance and either declares the goal met or produces the next breakdown. This repeats up to the parent's iteration budget.
  - Pause, gates, and edits work between any two iterations.

Excluded: nested breakdowns more than two levels deep (capped for safety), and automatic merging of best-of candidates.

## Options and tradeoffs

| Option | Approach | Benefit | Effort | Risks | Reversibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Breakdown artifact creates child tasks; `parallel` on steps with copies and best-of modes | Fans out at both levels, and everything reuses the existing controls | Large | More runs mean more cost, bounded by limits and caps | High |
| B | Let the lead handle all decomposition (status quo) and only add step copies | Smaller | Medium | Design steps still cannot hand work to parallel execution | High |

## Decision

- Agent recommendation: A
- Selected option: A
- Decided by: Lead
- Rationale and evidence: A answers both of the user's questions and keeps every run inside the same controls: holds, gates, edits, and limits.
- Uncertainty and what would change the decision: if best-of candidates prove hard to compare from summaries, add a diff-summary artifact for each candidate.

## Acceptance and validation

- A breakdown step's valid items become child tasks. Invalid items are rejected with reasons in the parent's activity. Edits at a gate change which children get created.
- A parent step with `waitFor: "children"` starts only after every child has settled.
- Parallel copies run concurrently up to the provider limits. A pause stops every copy. A late result from a superseded copy is discarded.
- In best-of, the chooser's selection is recorded, and only the chosen change reaches later steps and integration.
- Checks: typecheck, tests, build, and a browser pass.
