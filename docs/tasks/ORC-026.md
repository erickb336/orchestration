## Problem and outcome

**The problem.** The owner reviews results and artifacts, not the work as it happens. Agents' outputs are designs, handoffs, findings, reports, verifications and the lead's replies. They are written for the next step, so they assume context the owner does not have.

**The outcome.** A principle, `contextualize-and-write-for-the-reader`, that tells every agent to:

- lead with the point;
- place the output in the bigger picture (the goal or part of the vision it serves, what came before, what comes next);
- state the problem and each decision with its options and its reason;
- say what is needed;
- keep evidence close;
- use the reader's words.

## Decision

- **Selected:** a service-added principle, rather than adding it to every step in every flow file. One place to change it, and no flow file churn.
- **The cap.** Raising the cap is cheaper than dropping other principles from implement steps.
