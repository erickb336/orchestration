What happens

  1. Reading. Three readers read at the same time: the parts, the rules (from the tests) and the words.
  2. Review. The lead opens a round in Vision. It asks you only about conflicts and important guesses.
  3. Baseline. Your Lock in makes it the baseline: in force and built. The factory has nothing to build until you change something.

It only reads. The readers use a read-only copy. The CLI runs in the project's container with no network. Nothing is written into the repository, and the vision stays on this computer.
