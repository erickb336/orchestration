Three questions across the screens
PE review (your idea).

The PE is a rigid principal engineer who judges every option on feasibility, scale and longevity, on the other provider than the designer. The designer revises until the PE agrees (up to three rounds); if they still disagree, you see the objection and can overrule it, recorded. The PE can ask the lead for probes: small Vision tasks that bring evidence, such as surveying how many city councils publish transcripts and video for Council Record, or extracting one council's minutes. Probes run within a Vision budget you set, never in the factory. On Autopilot, the PE makes the factory's trade-off calls (findings that ask for a decision, choices between options), each recorded with its reasons and reversible by you.

Sharing artifacts (researched).

Neither Claude's nor Codex's published pages can be shown inside our canvas: both refuse to be framed. Both can publish privately in one step, and their own tools do it without asking. So: the studio's local copy stays the source of truth; Share publishes a copy only when you approve that publish (first target: a Claude artifact page; later Codex Sites or a preview deploy); and agents in the studio (designer, PE) have native publishing turned off.

One provider leads Vision.

The research shows the artifact tools don't decide it, since neither embeds. Proposal: Claude leads and designs in Vision (you said you would mostly use Claude at first); PE review runs on Codex, so the check is independent. The factory keeps either provider as lead, as AGENTS.md requires.
