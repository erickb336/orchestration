Here is my first draft of the vision and the first studio round. I've opened round 1 on the experience, and nothing is built yet.

**What I understand so far**
- Weekend Trips is a small browser app, on desktop and phones, for a group of friends planning one weekend away.
- It covers three things: who is in, the day plan, and what it costs each person.
- The repository has no code (one tracked file, documents only), and no vision documents are attached. So everything below beyond your one-line idea is an assumption.

**Round 1: experience**
The designer will make the trip home screen, the one screen everyone opens, in two variants on desktop and mobile. The choice is real: should the trip read as a timeline of days, or as a dashboard of three cards (who, plan, costs)? You can mark, pin and pick between them.

**Suggestions you may not have considered**
- **Joining:** friends probably join from a shared link with no account. That decides how much of the app has to exist.
- **Costs:** "what it costs each" could mean an even split or tracking who paid what. Tracking is much bigger, so I'd start with a simple per-person estimate.
- **Non-goals:** booking, payments, maps and chat are out of scope for now (assumption).
